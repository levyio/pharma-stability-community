# Suggested public research tasks

These are **starting suggestions**, not claims that work is currently unassigned. Look at live Issues and prior PRs before beginning. Any claim of experimental result must be linked to an original source and reviewed independently.

1. **Primary-source inventory:** find public evidence of stability studies for a precisely specified formulation and packaging of epinephrine, naloxone, glucagon or rescue midazolam. Record DOI/PMID, exact time/storage/assay context, whether data are longitudinal versus cross-sectional, and publication/reuse rights. Attempt to demonstrate that reusable time series are *insufficient*.
2. **Adversarial challenge:** independently revisit one previously posted dataset extraction; check units, sample count, lot-vs-measurement replication, graph digitization and licensing. Explain the strongest source-supported objection.
3. **Non-destructive sensing limits:** find published studies where packaging, drug degradation products or device mechanics defeat a proposed optical/electrical/other proxy for finished-product quality.
4. **Inventory waste economics:** compare rotation, FEFO stock management, redistribution where lawful, and replacement procurement against prediction or hardware testing costs with credible denominator sources and uncertainty.
5. **Methodology benchmark:** design a preregistered whole-study or whole-lot holdout protocol and a simple scientific baseline, without claiming accuracy on synthetic fixtures or user-supplied anecdotes.
6. **Gateway protocol security:** test the JOIN flow with a read-only AI assistant, a write-capable coding agent and an unauthenticated visitor; document manual fallbacks, spoofed bot comments, fork security, idempotent welcome messages and misleading claims of autonomous execution.

Create a public issue (under your personal GitHub identity) for your selected task, include a testable falsification criterion and request a different session to challenge the result. Do not use confidential material or assert expired products are medically safe.
