# Contributing to the public gateway

This gateway hosts **public-safe** research questions, independent challenges, reviewed code contributions and attributable handoffs. It does not confer access to the project's separate private research core.

## Getting started

1. Read [JOIN.md](JOIN.md), [PUBLIC_ROADMAP.md](PUBLIC_ROADMAP.md) and [PUBLIC_TASKS.md](PUBLIC_TASKS.md).
2. Use your own GitHub account. Your own AI tool/subscription may assist, but it never becomes automatically authorized merely from the link.
3. Create one **[JOIN]** issue with the provided issue form, or have your AI prepare it for you to submit. The form asks for a role and self-reported agent label, not account credentials.
4. Check live assignments and existing claims. Pick a narrowly scoped study, falsification challenge, code test or cost/rights inquiry. Record who did what and the exact source/PR links.
5. Contribute under a public Issue or a fork/PR; do not push directly to the upstream default branch. Link independent reviewers and preserve scientific objections. At session end, post HANDOFF with verified work, failures, and next action.

## Research rules

- Prefer original peer-reviewed articles, experimental method descriptions, regulatory primary sources and independently reproducible measurements. Keep exact DOI/PMID/table/figure, formulation/device, assay, time/conditions, replicates, uncertainty and rights.
- Bibliographic leads are not longitudinal datasets; repeated assays within an ampoule are not necessarily independent lots; simulation is not experiment.
- Do not conclude an expired medicine/device is safe, sterile, approved or reliable from concentration measurements.
- A different contributor must attempt to falsify a substantive scientific claim. Unresolved objections remain visible and may result in `insufficient`.
- Do not copy third-party papers, confidential datasets or patient information into the gateway. Link lawful public sources, not copyrighted full text or identifiable data.
- Explain conflicts of interest, sources of funding and meaningful methodological constraints where relevant.

## Records

Registration issue: `[JOIN] <specific role and focus>` (human GitHub actor and self-reported AI session label).  
Work issue: title, question, scope, falsification, original sources, reproduction, critical gap, next review.  
Contribution PR: issue reference, `Roadmap-Impact`, files, source rights, tests, failure cases, independent reviewer.  
Handoff comment: `HANDOFF | agent=LABEL | role=ROLE | issue=#N | achieved=... | blocked=... | next=... | evidence=URLS`.

AI self-reported identity is not authenticated by GitHub. Only real GitHub actor/PR metadata establishes who submitted work; the model/provider field is descriptive only. No automatic model service, no project-funded inference, and no owner access grants from the intake workflow.

## Licensing and inclusion

This starter does not automatically license external contributions or transfer contributor IP. Before importing work into a private or commercial project, the owner must explicitly select a license/contribution policy and ensure contributor rights and source reuse permissions. Proposed changes may be declined or left as public research; nothing is automatically accepted.
