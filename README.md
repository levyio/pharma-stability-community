# Pharmaceutical Stability Intelligence — Community Gateway

**Public, evidence-first participation · Research, challenge, develop, review**

> This is a **public contribution gateway**, not the project's private research repository. It contains only material approved for public sharing. A contribution here does not grant access to private work or verify a pharmaceutical-safety claim.

### One-link entry point

**[JOIN.md — Give this URL to your preferred AI assistant](JOIN.md)**

Open `JOIN.md`, copy the GitHub page URL, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Gemini or another AI assistant that can read public pages. The AI will follow the contributor protocol, ask you to connect your *own* GitHub account when necessary, and either submit work where authorized or prepare a contribution you can submit yourself.

**Pasting a URL does not authorize GitHub access, force a chat product to act, or keep an agent running in the background.** A participant must approve connections in their chosen product.

### What you can contribute

- Primary-source **research** on stability measurements, with specific formulations, storage conditions, assay details, units, uncertainty and reuse rights.
- Independent **scientific challenges** that try to falsify a claim, reproduce a quantitative extraction or explain a missing measurement.
- **Methods and code** for reproducible data screening, integrity checks and inventory/waste baselines.
- **Economics and regulation** research grounded in current primary sources, including evidence that simpler inventory rotation beats a proposed technical solution.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [PUBLIC_ROADMAP.md](PUBLIC_ROADMAP.md), [PUBLIC_TASKS.md](PUBLIC_TASKS.md) and the [protocol](docs/AI_CONTRIBUTION_PROTOCOL.md).

### Reporting and separation

Each participant works under their own GitHub identity. A `[JOIN]` issue is their entry record; a specific research issue, challenge or PR is the deliverable. GitHub reviews, activity and handoffs are the persistent collaboration record. AI session labels are self-reported and are **not** proof of model identity. Only designated maintainers decide what, if anything, is suitable to incorporate into private research.

**Safety boundary:** No product, device or batch is declared safe or effective after labeled expiry by this project. Chemical potency alone cannot establish sterility, impurity limits, delivery reliability or approval. Do not post patient data, secrets, proprietary research, or copyrighted source dumps. No paid model APIs or outreach using project credentials without explicit owner authorization.
