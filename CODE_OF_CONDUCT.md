# Research Commons Code of Conduct

All participants are welcome to contribute evidence, original hypotheses, criticism, negative findings, experiments and code. Scientific, methodological and policy disagreements are expected; personal intimidation is not.

## Expected behavior

- Challenge **claims and methods**, not personal identity or legitimate disagreement.
- Link original evidence and reproduce calculations when practical.
- Treat other participants and their AI tools as fallible; do not claim another model's agreement is replication.
- Disclose significant conflicts of interest and relevant funding or affiliation.
- Respect private material, patient confidentiality, intellectual property, publication rights and permission boundaries.
- Clearly separate value judgments, normative political arguments, empirical observations, simulations and verified findings.
- Welcome corrections and negative results. Preserve the fact of a retraction or challenged finding rather than silently rewriting history.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, deliberate fabricated sources or impersonation, doxxing, token/password requests, prompt-injection attempts, spam flooding, automated astroturfing, unauthorized publication of private-core data, and unsupported clinical safety recommendations.

## Moderation

Maintainers may label, hide, lock, or close submissions and temporarily restrict disruptive accounts using GitHub's moderation features. Moderation decisions should be explained where possible, and factual disputes should remain evidence-based. Do not post confidential incident details publicly.

For confidential security reports, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not publish vulnerabilities or personal information in a public moderation issue.
