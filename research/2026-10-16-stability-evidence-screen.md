## Screening question and eligibility gates

**Question:** Which *lawfully available original studies* contain product-specific, repeated storage-time observations that might be suitable for screening or later modeling of **finished rescue products**, rather than disconnected cross-sectional measurements or technical assay replicates?

For each study separate: (1) pharmaceutical **formulation and container/device**; (2) **physical units** and manufacturing **lot** IDs; (3) duration/temperature groups and **sampling schedule**; (4) **measurement endpoint** (parent analyte, impurity, preservatives, emitted volume/mass, activation, sterility, in-tissue delivery); (5) **unit of independence** (separate lot, device, ampoule, bag, aliquot, chromatographic injection); (6) rights and **access to machine-readable values**; and (7) possible **held-out whole-lot** validation.

Definitions: **A time-course curve is not necessarily a longitudinal study of the same sealed unit.** Sacrificing distinct ampoules at each duration generates a condition-level series; repeated HPLC injections are *technical* replicates. Repeated aliquots from one bag share the same experimental unit; neither permits a generalization across independent manufacturing lots without additional sampling.

### Strongest falsification of a concentration-only quality proxy

- **Lai et al. 2019:** parent naloxone concentration remained approximately stable under tested heat/freeze conditions **while** under heat, the *preservative* paraben signal fell and its degradation product increased (**3% paraben degradation reported after 4 weeks**); under freeze–thaw, **white precipitate** appeared after roughly a week. This is an experimentally observed divergence of endpoints **in one Sandoz ampoule formulation**. It does **not** show the observed change was clinically harmful, that all naloxone formulations contain these excipients, or that device function was impaired.
- **Wood et al. 2024 is affirmative counterevidence against an overstrong mechanical-failure narrative:** all 24 expired EpiPen/Jext devices activated, and discharged-mass comparisons were not statistically significant. Small n (three devices per brand/condition), differing expiry dates, unobserved potency and no tissue delivery mean neither universal reliability nor equivalence can be concluded. The results must not be discarded merely because they conflict with a failure hypothesis.
- **Rachid et al. 2016** measured expelled *volume* and epinephrine *mass* under thermal stress as different attributes; it does not validate concentration alone as a substitute for emitted mass.
- FDA's [NARCAN 4 mg nasal spray shelf-life extension notice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-announces-shelf-life-extension-naloxone-nasal-spray) distinguishes a **manufacturer/product-specific authorized extension for newly manufactured products** from **retroactive extension of previously distributed lots**, which the announcement expressly does **not** grant. This is regulatory scope, not experimental validation for products in this screen.

### Data quality and uncertainty register

1. **Lai's Methods versus Fig. 1 caption differ:** Methods list **day 21**, but the figure caption enumerates 0, 1–7, 14, 28. Confirm actual day-21 measurement in supplementary/author data before constructing a 21-day row. **Do not impute.**
2. For Lai, an identified manufacturer **lot #GH3080** is one independent lot; three HPLC injections per sample are **not three lots**. Exact number of separately exposed ampoules at each time/arm and replicate variance must be independently confirmed.
3. For Wood, individual listed expiry dates are **not manufacturing lot IDs**. Multiple devices with the same expiry date are not proven from distinct lots.
4. For Carr, serial samples from one bag per storage × concentration condition cannot support a test-set of independent bags or between-lot generalization.
5. Some cited records are **abstract-only** (Rachid, 2020/2022 naloxone): missing formulation specifics, sample n/independence, full methods and reuse rights are marked unknown, not guessed.
6. **Rights:** Lai's published article is CC BY 4.0, with data-availability stating analyzed data can be requested. No third-party full texts, plots or copyrighted tables copied into this repo; original numerical data have **not** been downloaded or digitized.
7. **No calibration accuracy, clinical safety, expiration extension or in-patient dose conclusions.** This screen is NOT a comprehensive literature review or proof that qualifying data do not exist elsewhere.

### Independent replication challenge and next work

A **different contributor/session** should check the original full texts, distinguish figure-caption omissions from underlying data, determine whether separate physical ampoules per time/arm exist, request Lai's machine-readable measurements/metadata **with clear permission**, and independently screen at least one other commercial formulation/lot. Require pre-registered holdout of **entire independent manufacturing lots** before making out-of-sample predictive claims; if no held-out lots exist, mark **INSUFFICIENT DATA** instead of splitting aliquots or analytic replicates into random train/test sets.

**Proposed next protocol:** provenance key `(study DOI, brand/product, strength, formulation, container, manufacturing lot, physical unit, condition, exposure start/end, assay batch, endpoint, replicate type, source rights)`; require a stable unique physical-unit identifier or document why condition-level cross-sectional cohorts are being used. Every extracted value must point to a public original table/figure and carry extraction uncertainty.
