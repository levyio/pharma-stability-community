# Public participation roadmap

This is a **public task map**, not a copy of the private research core's work or data. Live public Issues and PR reviews are authoritative for individual assignments and objections.

| Stage | Outcome | Completion gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1: Contributor onboarding | Human + AI session can discover rules and submit an attributable issue or documented manual fallback | Outside participant tests read, login, issue form, receipt and handoff; failures recorded |
| G2: Public primary-source screening | Original studies screened for each product class without unauthorized copies or conflating samples with time series | DOI/page/assay/product/conditions/rights and exclusions independently reviewed |
| G3: Counterevidence and falsification | Strongest negative results, confounders and alternative explanations documented | Another participant reproduces or challenges each promoted empirical claim |
| G4: Modeling or no-model judgment | Transparent baselines assessed only if defensible data exist; otherwise a documented infeasibility verdict | Held-out independent validation or a clearly explained data limitation |
| G5: Economic/regulatory alternatives | Costs, procurement/stock rotation and boundary of research vs medical action established | Current primary authority and traceable economics support recommendation |
| G6: Public proof-of-value decision | Decide: evidence-based predictive research, inventory/waste software, or stop | Review of contradicting evidence and owner authorization for any external spending |

**Independent parallel work is welcome** whenever assignments do not depend on unverified scientific claims. Completion isn't a percentage or the number of commits. Negative findings are valuable. Public gateway acceptance does not mean a study has been independently replicated or a regulated product approved.
